# YouTube Engine — Viral Hooks, Topics & Voice Playbook

This is your operating manual for getting Shorts to actually go viral on Indian YouTube
in 2026. Everything here is wired into the engine — you just need to apply the templates
when writing job files.

---

## PART 1 — The viral hook framework (first 1–3 seconds is everything)

Indian Shorts that consistently break 100k views on faceless content open with one of
these **7 hook types**. Mix and match per video; never repeat the same hook two days in a row.

### 1. Shock claim (the most reliable)
Open with a number or fact that feels impossible.
- "ChatGPT 2 minute me resume bana deta hai." (your current example)
- "₹500 ka SIP 30 saal me 1 crore. Maths galat nahi hai."
- "Yeh banda 19 saal ka hai aur company ki valuation ₹400 crore hai."

### 2. Pattern interrupt question
A question that forces the viewer to wait for the answer.
- "Tumhara phone tumhari baaten sun raha hai? Proof yahan hai."
- "Salary aate hi gayab kyu ho jaati hai? 80% Indians ki yahi galti hai."

### 3. "Ruko / Stop" command
Pulls people out of scroll mode. Works because it's interruptive.
- "Ruko. Resume bhejne se pehle yeh padh lo."
- "Stop scrolling. Yeh AI tool tumhara saara kaam khatam kar dega."

### 4. Curiosity gap (loop opening)
State the result, hide the method. Brain stays to close the loop.
- "Maine isse 3 minute me sundar website bana di. Method last me hai."
- "Yeh trick 99% logon ko nahi pata. Last clip dekho."

### 5. Mistake / fear hook
Loss aversion is the strongest emotion on social.
- "Yeh ek galti tumhare 5 lakh ka loss kara sakti hai."
- "ATS resume galat banaya toh interview kabhi nahi aayega."

### 6. Before/After / Transformation
Visually loud, high retention.
- "Pehle: 2 ghante. Aab: 2 minute. Kaise?"
- "₹0 se ₹10 lakh — yeh founder ne sirf 6 mahine me kiya."

### 7. Conspiracy / "they don't want you to know"
Polarising, but extremely shareable. Use sparingly to avoid burnout.
- "Banks tumhe yeh nahi batate. Issi se woh paisa banaate hain."
- "ChatGPT ke yeh 5 features Google se chhupa rahe hain."

### Hook formulas (drop into the `voice` field)

```
[Hook]. [Pattern interrupt]. [Promise]. [Steps 1-3]. [Payoff]. [CTA].
```

Ideal duration: **15–22 seconds** total. Hook = first 1.5s. Promise = next 1.5s. Steps =
8–12s. Payoff = 1–2s. CTA = 1–2s.

```bash
# permanent change for a single job: add to the job json
#   "voice_overrides": { "speaker": "vidya", "pace": 1.05 }
```

### Pace + temperature

- `pace`: 0.9 (calm finance) → 1.0 (default) → 1.08 (energetic AI hooks)
- `temperature`: 0.55 (consistent, stable) → 0.7 (more expressive, slightly variable)
  - Higher temp = more emotion but also more risk of weird inflection. Stick near 0.6.

### Pronunciation tips (write the script better, get a better voice)

- Write big numbers in words: "ek crore" not "1 crore"; "pachees hazaar" not "25,000"
- Use English brand names in Latin: ChatGPT, Zerodha, Mamaearth — Bulbul handles them
- Add commas where you'd breathe — Bulbul honours punctuation aggressively
- Use `?` for questions and `...` for dramatic pauses (3 dots, not 4)
- For mispronounced words, spell phonetically in Devanagari (e.g. "रिज्यूमे" for "resume")

## PART 4 — Music + SFX setup (now zero-touch)

You don't need to download anything anymore. The engine generates SFX automatically and
ships with a synthetic ambient BGM. To upgrade to real BGM later (recommended for production):

### Quick path (5 minutes per channel)

1. Open `studio.youtube.com` → left sidebar → **Audio Library**
2. Filter by: **Genre** → match the channel
   - AI Tadka: **Electronic** + **Cinematic**
   - Paisa Pathshala: **Jazz** + **Cinematic** (calm, confident)
   - Dhandha Dimaag: **Cinematic** + **Inspirational**
3. Filter by **Attribution** → **No attribution required** (safest)
4. Sort by length: pick a track ≥ 30 seconds
5. Download as MP3
6. Save to `channels/<name>/music/bgm.mp3` (replace the synthetic one)

### What's already auto-generated

- `channels/<name>/music/sfx_whoosh.mp3` — transition whoosh (synthesized, ~12 KB)
- `channels/<name>/music/sfx_ding.mp3` — payoff ding at the end (~7 KB)
- `channels/ai-tadka/music/bgm.mp3` — synthetic ambient drone (~480 KB, no beats)

### Why beat-sync currently does nothing

Synthetic ambient BGM has no percussive beats — librosa's beat tracker correctly returns
0 beats and the engine skips beat-snap. The moment you swap in a real percussive track
from YouTube Audio Library (anything with drums or a kick), beat-sync activates
automatically and snaps your scene cuts to the BGM beat.

## PART 5 — The script-writing template (copy-paste this)

Use this every time you write a job's `voice` field. ~28-30 seconds is the sweet spot.

```
[Hook — 1 sentence, max 8 words. One of the 7 hook types above.]
[Pattern interrupt — 1 short sentence. Question or shock.]
[Promise — what they'll get if they watch. 1 sentence.]
[Step 1 — the actual how-to. 1-2 short sentences.]
[Step 2 — the actual how-to. 1-2 short sentences.]
[Step 3 — the actual how-to OR the reveal. 1-2 short sentences.]
[Payoff — emotion + result. 1 sentence.]
[CTA — Follow X for daily Y.]
```

### And the captions (5 max, one per scene):

| # | kicker | line | broll |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | RUKO / STOP | The shock or question | matches mood (frustration, confusion) |
| 2 | STEP 1 | First how-to in 5 words | matches action |
| 3 | STEP 2 | Second how-to in 5 words | matches action |
| 4 | STEP 3 / TIP | Third how-to or twist | matches action |
| 5 | DONE / RESULT | The payoff in 5 words | success / happy outcome |

### Caption micro-rules
- Each `line` ≤ 6 words
- Wrap the **payoff word** in `*asterisks*` so it pulses in the accent colour
- Use real emojis in `icon` (🤯, ⚡, 💸, 📈, ✅, 🚀)
- `broll` should be a **short English search term** (Pexels searches in English)

## PART 6 — What the engine now does for you (so you don't have to)

When you run `python scripts/make_video.py --job <path>`:

1. ✅ Synthesizes voice with your channel's preferred Sarvam speaker
2. ✅ Transcribes the VO with Whisper for **word-level timing**
3. ✅ Aligns the original Hinglish script to Whisper's timings (no more Urdu captions!)
4. ✅ Snaps scene starts to actual word boundaries
5. ✅ Detects beats in your BGM and snaps cuts to the beat (when BGM has beats)
6. ✅ Fetches real Pexels stock B-roll per scene (one moving clip behind each)
7. ✅ Adds whoosh SFX on each scene cut + ding on the payoff
8. ✅ Fires a giant emoji "reaction burst" on each scene (CapCut-style visual SFX)
9. ✅ Cycles 4 transition variants (whip pan, zoom punch, glitch shake, 3D flip)
10. ✅ Self-hosts fonts so typography is always crisp
11. ✅ Renders to 1080×1920 30fps with hardware-accelerated browser GPU
12. ✅ Normalizes audio to YouTube's -14 LUFS standard

You write 1 JSON file. The engine does steps 1-12. You review the MP4 and upload.
